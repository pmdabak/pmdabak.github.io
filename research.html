Research

Projects, fieldwork, and notes from along the way.

Project Sept 2024 – present

Language Allocation in Practice in Dual Language Classrooms

with Dr. Jennifer Renn

A case study examining bilingual teachers' micro-interactions and language choices in a Spanish–English dual language program, asking how they articulate and enact language allocation policy in the classroom. Data include classroom observation videos from a larger federally-funded project and stimulated recall interviews where teachers reflect on their language decisions.

Project Jan 2024

Conceptual Metaphors of Multilingualism in Marathi

with Dr. Jessica Chandras

An independent research project exploring how urban speakers from Maharashtra expressed their language ideologies around Marathi Language Day. Using conceptual metaphor as a lens, we examined how language ideologies are embedded in representations of language, multilingualism, and education.
