Projects, fieldwork, and notes from along the way.

Project Ongoing

Language Allocation in Practice in Dual Language Classrooms

with Dr. Jennifer Renn

A case study examining bilingual teachers' micro-interactions and language choices in a Spanish–English dual language program, asking how they articulate and enact language allocation policy in the classroom. Data include classroom observation videos from a larger federally-funded project and stimulated recall interviews where teachers reflect on their language decisions.

Project Ongoing

Interactional and Questioning Moves in Instructional Coaching Discourse

with Dr. Haiyan Li and Dr. Jennifer Renn

A study of the discourse strategies, interactional moves and questions, used by a sociocultural instructional coach. We combine qualitative methods with computational tools to perform close coaching discourse analysis and to apply sentence embeddings for uncovering patterns across a larger dataset.

Moment May 2025

Translinguistic Institute at Penn State

My autoethnography of translanguaging and intercultural communication as a tourist in Hampi, India, was accepted in this unique institute where I spent a week "re-visioning" the paper, with feedback from (the) Dr. Suresh Canagarajah. It was like a conference + getaway + writer's retreat, with scholars around the world.

Project Oct 2024

Academic and Ecological Congruence in Dual Language Bilingual Education

with a team led by Dr. Trish Morita-Mullaney

This project examined the longitudinal impact of ESL coursework, coaching, and teacher demographics on dual language teachers' academic instruction and classroom environment, using the CLASS observation tool and the Purdue Dual Language Framework.

Moment August 2024

First Time in a US Classroom

My fellowship year was about "exploring." For me, that meant getting into classrooms as soon as I could. I volunteered as a research assistant on my advisor's grant and went for data collection, getting my first glimpse of bilingual education in the US context. The culture shock was... shocking. I'd been a school teacher my whole career but the "hidden curriculum" of Indian schooling that I've carried in me was both affirmed and challenged, and in just one visit.

Moment July 2023

First Fieldwork Experience

In June 2023, I attended a webinar on linguistic ethnography in rural Maharashtra, and cold-emailed the speaker asking if I could join her for fieldwork! I didn't expect that this experience and her mentorship would rearrange what I wanted to study. A month later, I scrapped my shortlist of psycholinguistics programs and applied to bilingual education programs instead.

Project Feb 2023

Conceptual Metaphors of Multilingualism in Marathi

with Dr. Jessica Chandras

February 27th is Marathi Language Day, and that brings with it a lot of talk about linguistic pride. I started this as an independent project to explore the conceptual metaphors undergirding these discourses. Eventually, it morphed into a collaborative analysis of how urban Marathi speakers conceptualize language ideologies about multilingualism and language education.
